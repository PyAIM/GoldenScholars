    Latinized names

    European texts knew several of these scholars under Latin names: Algorismi/Algoritmi (al-Khwarizmi), Albategnius (al-Battani), Alhazen (Ibn al-Haytham), Anaritius (al-Nayrizi), Avicenna (Ibn Sina), Azarquiel (al-Zarqali), Geber (Jabir ibn Aflah), Alfraganus (al-Farghani, astronomer, mentioned in the transmission chapters), Arzachel (al-Zarqali variant), Thebit (Thabit ibn Qurra).
